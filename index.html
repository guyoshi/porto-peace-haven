House Rules

We ask all guests to respect these guidelines so everyone enjoys a calm, welcoming stay

🤫

Noise & Quiet Hours

  • Please keep noise to a minimum at all times.
  • Be especially considerate on weekdays and during business hours.
  • No loud music or raised voices inside the property.
🚭

No Smoking

  • No smoking or vaping anywhere inside the building.
  • Smoking is allowed outside the building only.
  • Candles and incense are not permitted — smoke detectors are active.
👥

Visitors & Events

  • No unregistered visitors are permitted on the premises.
  • Parties and social gatherings are strictly not allowed.
🍽️

Kitchen & Shared Areas

  • Always wash dishes immediately after use.
  • Clean the kitchen surfaces and hob after every use.
  • Treat shared areas with care and consideration for others.
💡

Before Leaving Your Room

  • Close all windows before you leave.
  • Switch off all lights.
  • Turn off all appliances (fan, TV, etc.).
🚿

Bathroom

  • Always verify the toilet flush has stopped completely before leaving.
  • Only toilet paper may be flushed — no wipes or other items.
  • Leave the bathroom clean and ready for the next guest.
🐾

Pets

  • Pets are not permitted at Porto Peace Haven.
🪑

Furniture & Floors

  • Please do not drag chairs or furniture across the floors.
  • Handle all furnishings with care.

Thank you for helping us keep Porto Peace Haven a peaceful, welcoming space for everyone. 🙏

Kitchen Guide

How to use everything in the kitchen

🍳

Induction Hob

  1. Place a compatible (magnetic) pan on the hob surface.
  2. Press the power button to switch on.
  3. Select your zone and adjust heat with the + / – controls.
  4. The hob switches itself off automatically after 2 hours.
💨

Extractor Hood

  1. Switch on before cooking to prevent smoke build-up.
  2. Adjust speed with the button (1 = low · 3 = high).
  3. Leave running briefly after cooking, then switch off.
🥐

Mini Oven

  1. Place food on the tray or rack provided.
  2. Set temperature using the left dial.
  3. Set cooking time using the right dial.
  4. Oven switches off automatically when timer ends.
📡

Microwave

  1. Use only microwave-safe containers.
  2. Set time and power, then press start.
  3. Never use metal containers or foil.
🫖

Kettle

  1. Fill with water up to the maximum line indicated inside.
  2. Place on the base and press the switch down.
  3. It switches off automatically once boiled.
☕

Capsule Coffee Machine

  1. Fill the water tank and switch the machine on.
  2. Insert a compatible capsule into the holder.
  3. Place your cup and press brew.
  4. Remove and dispose of used capsule after each use.
🍴

What's Available

Plates, bowls, glasses, mugs, cups, cutlery, pots, pans, cutting boards, knives, colander, grater, and cooking utensils.

Cleaning Supplies

What to Do in Porto

Our favourite recommendations for your stay

🌅
🌅 Sunset

Best Sunset Spots

Jardim do Morro in Vila Nova de Gaia offers spectacular views over the Douro and Dom Luís Bridge at golden hour. Miradouro da Serra do Pilar is equally breathtaking.

Go →
📸
📸 Photography

Best Photo Spots

Dom Luís I Bridge, the iconic Livraria Lello, the Ribeira waterfront, the blue tiles of São Bento Station, and the colourful houses of Bonfim are all unmissable.

Go →
🌧️
🌧️ Rainy Day

Rainy Day Plan

Livraria Lello bookshop, Casa da Música, the Serralves Museum of Contemporary Art, or a long slow lunch at a traditional tasca. Porto's café culture was made for rainy days.

Go →
🗺️
🗺️ Classic

Classic Porto Itinerary

São Bento Station → Sé Cathedral → Ribeira waterfront → Dom Luís Bridge → Port wine cellar tasting in Gaia → sunset at Jardim do Morro. A perfect day in one loop.

Go →
🍷
🍷 Food & Wine

Food & Wine

A Francesinha at Café Santiago, pastel de nata at a local padaria, bacalhau à Brás, and finish with a port wine tasting at Graham's or Taylor's in Gaia. Porto eats well.

Go →
🏖️
🏖️ Beach

Coastal & Beach Day

Matosinhos beach is just 15 minutes by metro — ideal for swimming, fresh seafood at the harbour restaurants, and big Atlantic skies. Foz do Douro is lovely for a riverside walk.

Go →
